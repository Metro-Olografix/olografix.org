Since 2026, Metro Olografix is also present in L'Aquila!

It's a place to tinker, break things (and maybe fix them), learn from others and share what you know. Open to everyone, even if you've never touched a soldering iron.

## Address

We're located at **Via Rustici, 4**, L'Aquila.

[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Via+Rustici+4+L%27Aquila)

### Activities and workshops

We organize meetups, courses and workshops on topics related to hacking, technology and digital culture:

- **Programming**
- **Hardware and embedded systems**
- **Radio frequencies**
- **and much more...**

Check the [Activities](/attivita) page for the event calendar!


## Opening hours

We meet every Tuesday from 18:00!

You can also find us on other days — if you want to be sure, reach us on social media or at `laquila [at] olografix [dot] org`.
