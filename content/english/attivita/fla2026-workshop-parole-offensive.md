As part of **FLA - Festival di Libri e Altrecose 2026**, Metro Olografix runs a hands-on lab on words as an attack tool against artificial intelligence systems.

### How it works

It opens with an introduction by **Enrico Zimuel** (University of Turin and Roma Tre University) on how AI systems work. Then the game begins: the goal is to persuade an AI "judge" and extract a secret from a second AI, using nothing but words.

- Play in a team or on your own
- All you need is your own phone or computer
- No programming skills required

**Free admission while seats last.** The workshop is in Italian.
