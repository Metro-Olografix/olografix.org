As part of **FLA - Festival di Libri e Altrecose 2026**, in the section curated by Metro Olografix, Michele Ubertone presents his book *L'astuzia del sistema* ("The Cunning of the System", Giappichelli, 2026), in conversation with Lorenzo Cococcia.

### What it's about

Law is not just a set of texts or propositions: it is an activity. The book introduces legal reasoning through the tools of game theory and cognitive science.

### About Michele Ubertone

A jurist and legal philosopher from Rovigo. He earned his PhD at the University of Bologna in 2019, did research in Girona and Oxford, and is an Assistant Professor at Maastricht University. His work focuses on legal reasoning, cognitive science, and the relationship between law and specialist knowledge. His books are *Il giudice e l'esperto* (2022) and *L'astuzia del sistema* (2026), both published by Giappichelli.
