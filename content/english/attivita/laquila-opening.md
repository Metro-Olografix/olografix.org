After 32 years of experimentation, Metro Olografix is opening a new space in L'Aquila.

It's at Via Ed Arco Cirillo 10, in the heart of the historic center. A place that didn't exist until yesterday, in an area that has never had anything like it.

### Why a physical space?

Because the most interesting things happen when people actually meet in person. A space for hacking, making and knowledge sharing, where you take things apart to understand how they work and build new ones. An open, inclusive and democratic gathering point: what matters is not what you know, but the curiosity you bring.

If you know our Pescara headquarters, you already know what to expect. If you don't, that's a good reason to come find out.

### What happens at the opening?

We open the doors, show you the space and tell you what we're going to do with it. We'll bring something to drink and eat — you bring faces and curiosity.

This is day zero of a new adventure.
